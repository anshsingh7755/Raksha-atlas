# Indian Defence Future Programmes & Acquisition Intelligence Dossier

**Research cut-off:** 23 November 2026  
**Repository branch:** `work/step-1-foundation`  
**Scope:** Publicly available Indian Army, Navy, Air Force, joint and strategic programmes that are planned, proposed, under design/development, undergoing trials, in an approval pipeline, tendered, contracted or under construction.

> **Research standard:** This is a discovery and evidence dossier, not an official government order of battle or a claim that every programme has been found. It deliberately includes official records and unofficial open-source reporting, but labels them separately. No unverified quantity, specification, approval or schedule is upgraded to fact. The user-requested IDRW material is treated as a secondary reporting stream, not as an official source.

## 1. Executive summary

This dossier records **46 programme entries** from this research pass, spanning strategic missiles, hypersonic technology, land systems, naval platforms, submarines, combat aircraft, radars, air defence, drones and support systems.

The source register has been expanded to include official PIB/DRDO/BrahMos Aerospace references and IDRW discovery/reporting references. IDRW is useful for discovering claims and following programme reporting, but claims based on unnamed sources or editorial estimates remain **reported / unverified** until confirmed by MoD, PIB, DRDO, service headquarters, Parliament, the named developer or shipyard.

### Evidence classes used

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Official** | Direct government, service, DRDO, named developer or official programme source |
| **Official test milestone** | Officially confirmed test or technology milestone; not necessarily a production order |
| **Unofficial reporting** | IDRW or other secondary report; may rely on unnamed sources |
| **Mixed** | Official programme existence plus unofficial progress, quantity or schedule claims |
| **Unconfirmed concept** | No public official approval or programme sanction found in this research pass |

### Acquisition stages used

- **Concept / requirement:** a stated need or design concept; no approval implied.
- **AoN:** Acceptance of Necessity; in-principle administrative approval, not a contract.
- **RFI / RFP / tender:** market survey or solicitation; not a purchase.
- **Development / prototype:** design, fabrication or prototype phase.
- **Trials:** test milestone; not automatic user clearance or production.
- **Contract signed / ordered:** procurement commitment; not active service.
- **Construction / production:** industrial execution; not delivered fleet.
- **Delivered / commissioned / in service:** only after a source explicitly confirms the relevant milestone.

## 4. Programme distinctions that must not be merged

1. **Agni-VI vs Project Vishnu vs hypersonic glide / LR-AShM:** these are different public claims and technology/programme lines. Agni-VI has no official approval confirmation in the IDRW article; Project Vishnu is reported as awaiting final development approval; official scramjet ground-test milestones do not by themselves confirm either named missile.
2. **BrahMos-NG vs existing BrahMos:** BrahMos-NG is a next-generation derivative in development; do not add its planned quantities to the existing BrahMos inventory.
3. **INS Vishal / IAC-III vs second IAC-1 carrier:** the former is the larger CATOBAR concept; the latter is reported as a possible repeat Vikrant-class proposal. Keep them separate.
4. **Zorawar initial phase vs later light-tank competition:** the reported 59 initial requirement is not the same as the reported 295 competitive follow-on or a 354 total planning figure. Do not treat the latter as ordered.
5. **Project-76 vs Project-77 vs Project-75I:** Project-76 is reported as a conventional submarine design line; Project-77 as a nuclear-powered attack submarine line; Project-75I is a separate conventional submarine acquisition programme.
6. **Project Kusha land system vs reported naval adaptation:** a successful land-system flight test does not prove ship fitment or a naval contract.
7. **AMCA prototypes vs serial fighter production:** prototype-development procurement is not a production order or squadron quantity.
8. **Pinaka LRGR test vs launcher/rocket procurement:** successful test is a milestone, not a new order quantity.
9. **AIP module development vs submarine procurement:** the AIP programme is a subsystem development/integration effort, not a new submarine class.
10. **C-295 MMMA/MRMR:** keep the reported Navy and Coast Guard aircraft allocations separate; the Coast Guard component is not part of the Navy fleet.

## 5. IDRW research methodology and limitations

IDRW is included because it is a useful open-source defence-news discovery stream and the user explicitly requested it. The site contains:
- News Beat articles;
- reports attributed to named writers or unnamed sources;
- republished official releases;
- AFI-branded articles;
- forecasts and expected-clearance roundups;
- technical and programme commentary.

These categories are not equivalent. Every IDRW article must be tagged with its own provenance:
- **IDRW original / sources close to programme:** unofficial secondary reporting;
- **IDRW republished PIB:** trace back to original PIB and cite PIB as primary;
- **AFI / editorial:** secondary or opinion-style reporting;
- **forecast / expected milestone:** not evidence that the milestone occurred.

The archive is large and changing. This pass has used selected programme-specific articles and the News Beat archive as a discovery index. It has **not** downloaded, read or exhaustively indexed every page or attachment on idrw.org. Therefore, this document is an initial research dossier, not a claim of exhaustive IDRW coverage.

## 7. Data model recommended for the next inventory ingestion

Every programme should have:
- canonical programme name and aliases;
- service/domain and system category;
- parent programme and sub-programme links;
- official programme existence status;
- acquisition lifecycle stage and stage date;
- quantity, quantity unit and quantity scope;
- manufacturer / developer / shipyard / consortium;
- prototype count separately from production requirement;
- AoN, RFI, RFP, tender, CCS, contract, construction, trial, delivery and induction milestones;
- source URL, source publication date, source class and exact quoted/parsed claim;
- confidence level and verification status;
- contradictory claims and reconciliation notes;
- last reviewed date.

**Critical:** never convert an article's forecast into a milestone. Never convert an unnamed-source estimate into an official quantity. Never treat a test as an order or a design as a sanctioned programme.
